About this edition

This edition is part of Bibliothecarius Modernus, an independent digital library of neglected Latin literature. Every edition is complete, shows the Latin beside the English so that the translation can be checked, and states its source, its method and its review status here. These are not critical editions, but they are documented, source-linked digital editions with explicit provenance and review status.

The same edition can be heard on the Internet Archive, watched on YouTube, and cloned from the open text corpus on GitHub (links above). Software can find it in the machine-readable catalog{% if catalog_id %} under {{ catalog_id }}{% endif %}.

Translators and scholars are welcome to build on this work: the translation, introduction and metadata are dedicated to the public domain (CC0 1.0). No permission is needed; acknowledgment is appreciated.
