  <p>Bibliothecarius Modernus publishes Latin Christian and medieval texts with English translations. It focuses on works that have not been translated before, are available only in older or hard-to-find translations, or have received little attention outside specialist scholarship.</p>

  <p>Each edition brings the Latin and English together, with an introduction, bibliography, and downloadable text and metadata. Many also include audio and video. I generally aim to publish complete works where the source permits. If only part of a work survives, the edition identifies what is missing.</p>

  <p>The Latin usually comes from digitized editions, including older printed collections. Some works require a transcription from a digitized manuscript before they can be translated. Each edition identifies the source used and documents any significant uncertainties.</p>

  <h3 id="how-the-translations-are-made">How the translations are made</h3>

  <p>AI helps with translation, research, and production. I choose the texts and sources, check coverage and references, review the output, and make corrections before publication. The Latin is published alongside the English so that readers who know the language can assess the translation for themselves. These publications are not critical editions, and I do not claim that a single source or manuscript establishes a definitive text.</p>

  <h3>Open for reuse</h3>

  <p>The project's translations, research, metadata, and original transcriptions are released under <a href="https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/" target="_blank">CC0</a>. The historical Latin texts are public domain. The material is available for teaching, research, new translations, datasets, and other projects. See <a href="{{ '/LICENSE.md' | relative_url }}">Licensing</a> for details.</p>
